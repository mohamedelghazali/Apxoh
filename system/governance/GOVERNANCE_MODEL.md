# GOVERNANCE MODEL

## Governing principles

1. Every requirement has measurable acceptance criteria.
2. Every rule has a stable identifier, scope, rationale, severity, and verification method.
3. CI success alone does not prove quality.
4. Security claims require reproducible verification evidence.
5. Performance claims require defined workload and measurement methodology.
6. Reliability claims require failure and recovery testing.
7. Production release requires documented readiness review.
8. Critical unresolved risks block release unless a valid policy explicitly permits an exception.
9. Changes require impact analysis and review.
10. Findings require remediation evidence and retesting.
11. Quality and security are monitored after release.
12. Decisions are based on evidence, not confidence or opinion.

## Decision statuses

PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED, NOT_APPLICABLE, ACCEPTED_RISK. BLOCKED and NOT_APPLICABLE are never silently converted to PASS.
